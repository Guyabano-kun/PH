How to plan a trip to Boracay

A structured plan helps avoid situations where a cheap flight leads to an expensive transfer or a hotel is far from the part of the island you intend to visit.

Plan step by step

1. Choose dates and season

Check the Amihan/Habagat season, wind, rainfall and conditions on your preferred coast.

2. Book a flight

Compare MPH and KLO by total cost and travel time to your hotel, not only by airfare.

3. Book accommodation

Choose an area and check actual walking access, total price and cancellation terms.

4. Arrange transfers

Check the airport–port–island–hotel route, especially if arriving late.

5. Prepare payments and connectivity

Prepare cash, cards, QR Ph, SIM/eSIM and mobile registration.

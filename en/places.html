Main visitor areas

White Beach

Beachfront promenade

The island's main visitor-facing shoreline, used for seaside walks, beach time, restaurants and sunset viewing. Sea conditions change with the season.

D'Mall

Shopping and dining area

A central reference area for shops, cafés, restaurants and everyday purchases. Check the exact location of each venue and the route from your accommodation.

Mount Luho

Viewpoint area

One of the island's known viewpoint areas. Before travelling, confirm actual access, opening arrangements and visiting conditions with the operator or locally.

Bulabog

Eastern side of the island

A coast associated with wind-driven water sports. Beach selection and seasonal limitations are covered in the beach passports and weather guide.

Map and geographic accuracy

Not every tourist reference is automatically a verified map point. A beach, entrance, facility and viewpoint are different geographic feature types. Do not use a nearby marker as a substitute for an exact location.

SIM, eSIM and internet on Boracay

For mobile data on the island, you can use a local Globe or Smart SIM/eSIM. The right option depends on device compatibility, trip duration, data needs and current plan availability.

Check before purchasing

Device compatibility

Check eSIM support and make sure the phone is not carrier-locked. On iPhone, check Settings → General → About → Carrier Lock; on Android, consult the manufacturer and carrier.

SIM registration

SIMs and eSIMs in the Philippines are subject to registration. Follow the carrier's current requirements and use its official registration process.

Island coverage

Coverage and speed may differ between White Beach, Bulabog, Yapak, inland areas and inside buildings. A general coverage map is not a guarantee of signal in a specific room.

Validity

Check plan validity, renewal rules, remaining data, calls and texts. Tourist bundles and regular prepaid plans may have different terms.

Home internet and long-term stays

For stays of a month or longer, check fixed broadband and fixed wireless availability at the exact accommodation address. A provider serving the area does not guarantee service in a particular building.

Providers and utilities

Plans, promotions, eSIM availability and registration requirements change. Verify current information with Globe or Smart before paying. PH does not sell SIM/eSIM products or guarantee signal quality at a specific location.

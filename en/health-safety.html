Practical precautions

Sea and water activities

  • Check PAGASA advisories and local authority instructions.
  • Do not go to sea when authorities prohibit it or the operator cancels the trip.
  • Use a life jacket where required by the activity rules.
Weather, wind and sea →

Sun and heat

  • Use sun protection and maintain fluid intake.
  • Plan strenuous activities with heat and humidity in mind.
  • Stop the activity if you feel unwell and seek medical assistance.

Transport and personal belongings

  • Wear a helmet and check rental conditions when using two-wheeled transport.
  • Keep documents and valuables separate from beach belongings.
  • Check lighting and the actual route when travelling at night.

This is general information, not medical advice or a complete emergency-services directory. Hospital contact details and service availability may change; verify contacts with official sources before travelling. In an emergency, do not delay seeking help to check this website.
